Tolerates — frame loss in a single sweep
Frame timing is expressed in whole display refreshes, not milliseconds — a QR that changes mid-refresh produces a torn frame the camera cannot decode. At 60 Hz, 5 refreshes = 83.3 ms = 12 fps.

Transmit area matters more than anything else on a wide panel. Codes spread across a 32:9 screen force the phone to letterbox, throwing away two thirds of the sensor. Painting into a centred block whose aspect matches the camera lets the operator fill the frame with codes instead of black bars.
